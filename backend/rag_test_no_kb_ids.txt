answer=## Payment Methods

- **Credit cards** – We accept all major credit cards, including Visa, MasterCard, and American Express [1, 3].

- **PayPal** – PayPal is accepted as an alternative online payment option [1, 3].

- **Invoice** – Enterprise customers can pay via invoice [1, 3].

- **Bank transfer** – Enterprise customers also have the option to pay by bank transfer [3, 4].
